## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, structured, and modern civic enterprise aesthetic engineered for regional administrative civil servants and cooperative operators across West Java. 

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Institutional Authority with Contemporary Clarity:** Replaces dense, bureaucratic software clutter with crisp architectural alignment, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and unambiguous operational states.
- **Precision & High Data Density:** Balances high-volume record auditing (cooperative legal metrics, SHU, assets, membership status) with low cognitive fatigue through calculated whitespace and scannable visual anchors.
- **Trust & Civic Responsibility:** Emits state-level credibility through deep navy foundation surfaces, warm gold ceremonial insignia, and high-legibility tabular reporting.

### Design Movement: Modern Civic Enterprise
The visual language combines the systematic rigor of enterprise data platforms with refined governmental prestige. Visual noise is eliminated; layout balance, deliberate low-contrast perimeter dividers, and functional state tokens drive interaction clarity rather than decorative embellishment.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative formality with immediate status comprehension.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2942`):** Grounding foundation for persistent shell elements, sidebars, page headers, primary operational triggers, and heavy typography. Deepened to `#001428` for high-density app shells and contrasting command bars.
- **Secondary Action Blue (`#2563EB`):** Applied strictly to dynamic interactive controls, focused inputs, table pagination triggers, inline hyperlinks, and actionable batch controls.
- **Civic Accent Amber/Gold (`#D97706`):** Reserved for statutory accreditation seals, regulatory warnings, validation queues, and verified provincial tier markers.
- **Neutral Structure Slate (`#64748B`):** Governs structural borders (`#E2E8F0`), subtle neutral surfaces (`#F8FAFC`), card backdrops (`#FFFFFF`), and subdued metadata typography (`#64748B` to `#334155`).

### Operational Status Tokens
- **Disetujui / Aktif (Success):** `#059669` (Text/Icon on `#ECFDF5` container, `#A7F3D0` stroke).
- **Menunggu Verifikasi (Pending):** `#D97706` (Text/Icon on `#FFFBEB` container, `#FDE68A` stroke).
- **Perlu Perbaikan / Ditolak (Critical):** `#E11D48` (Text/Icon on `#FFF1F2` container, `#FECDD3` stroke).

## Typography

Typographic scale is powered exclusively by Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain a clean, approachable, yet formal government tone with optimal legibility across tabular datasets and document viewports.

### Typographic Implementation Rules
- **Numerical Tabular Data:** All financial amounts (IDR/Rupiah), Nomor Induk Koperasi (NIK), and dates must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to maintain strict vertical alignment across repeating table rows.
- **Section & Document Titles:** Use `headline-sm` and `title-md` with `font-weight: 600` in deep navy (`#0F2942`) to assert hierarchical separation without excessive vertical spacing.
- **Administrative Badges & Column Headers:** Utilize `label-md` and `label-sm` with upper or title casing and subtle letter-spacing (`0.025em` - `0.04em`) to optimize scanning across dense data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to host multi-panel administrative workflows, filters, and dense tabular ledgers.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fixed collateral navigation sidebar (260px), 12-column body content grid, `2rem` (`margin`) outer padding, and `1.5rem` (`gutter`) column gaps.
- **Tablet / Notebook (768px - 1279px):** Collapsible sidebar rail (72px), 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins, and `1rem` column gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Off-canvas drawer, single-column stacked view, `1rem` (`margin-mobile`) outer margins, and `0.75rem` gutters. Metric cards stack in pairs or single modules.

### Internal Spacing Rhythm
- Form field controls, table cells, and button components adhere strictly to 4px/8px incremental boundaries.
- Metric cards leverage `space-lg` internal padding. Table cells use `space-md` horizontal padding and `space-sm` vertical padding for optimal desktop data scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system adopts a crisp, enterprise architectural depth strategy reliant on low-contrast structural outlines rather than heavy theatrical shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Background base layer rendered in Slate Clean (`#F8FAFC`). No borders or shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Core data cards, tables, and metric summaries rendered in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Enhanced with a faint ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Tooltips & Dropdowns):** Floating action menus, date pickers, and filter panels. Outlined in `#CBD5E1` with ambient depth: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Verification Drawers):** High-priority confirmation and audit dialogues. Outlined in `#E2E8F0` overlaid on an institutional dimming backdrop (`rgba(0, 20, 40, 0.5)`), layered with: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius, `0.5rem` / `8px` container radius, and `0.75rem` / `12px` major modal radius). 

### Application Guidelines
- **Input Fields, Table Search Bars, Select Menus:** `rounded` (`0.25rem` / `4px`) to preserve sharp administrative structure and high vertical density.
- **Cards, Alert Panels, Summary Metric Containers:** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`), balancing modern software clarity with institutional stability.
- **Status Badges & Verification Pills:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to visually differentiate administrative tags from interactive button modules.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Simpan Data, Buat Laporan):** Background `#0F2942`, text white, `rounded` (4px), padding `0.5rem 1rem`. Focus state features a 2px offset ring in `#2563EB`. Hover state `#1E3A5F`.
- **Secondary Action (Export Excel, Filter):** Background white, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F2942`. Hover state `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive Action (Tolak Verifikasi, Hapus):** Background `#FFF1F2`, border 1px solid `#FECDD3`, text `#E11D48`. Hover state `#FFE4E6`.

### 2. Metric Cards (Clean KPI Cards)
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, padding `1.25rem`, border-radius `0.5rem`.
- Layout: Subdued label on top (`label-md`, `#64748B`), large numeric tally in deep navy (`headline-md`, tabular nums, `#0F2942`), accompanied by an inline status delta or badge in the top right corner.

### 3. Dynamic Foreach Table Rows
- Enclosed in an outer container with border 1px solid `#E2E8F0` and `rounded-lg`.
- **Header (`<thead>`):** Background `#F8FAFC`, border-b 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-md` uppercase in `#475569`.
- **Row Iteration (`<tbody> tr`):** Subtle bottom divider (`#F1F5F9`). Alternating hover state with background `#F8FAFC`. Vertical padding on cells `0.75rem`, horizontal padding `1rem`. Font size `13px` or `14px`.

### 4. Badges (Status Administratif)
Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`, typography `label-sm` with an inline 6px indicator dot:
- **Disetujui / Aktif:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot `#059669`.
- **Menunggu Verifikasi:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot `#D97706`.
- **Perlu Perbaikan / Ditolak:** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#9F1239`, dot `#E11D48`.

### 5. Regulatory Policy Alert Box
Designed for official government decrees, instructions, or regulatory verification alerts:
- Outer box with border-l-4 in Amber (`#D97706`) or Government Blue (`#2563EB`).
- Background `#FFFBEB` (for statutory notices) or `#EFF6FF` (for general administrative guidance).
- Left-aligned provincial government icon followed by an authoritative header (`title-sm`) and concise policy guidance (`body-md`).

### 6. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Inputs & Selects:** Native appearance normalized with white background, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, `rounded` (4px), height 38px, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`. Focus: border color `#2563EB` with outline ring `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Accent color `#2563EB`, border `#94A3B8`, sharp feedback cues on active row multi-select tables.
